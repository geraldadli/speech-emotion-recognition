# Voice journal project criteria

Current purpose: help people who prefer speaking to typing record and reflect on
personal experiences. The workflow is speak, review/correct text, choose one's own
feeling, optionally save, and revisit entries. Model-estimated tone is a separate
reflection cue, never a diagnosis or a substitute for the user's own account.
This purpose and benefit still need validation with real target users.

The COMP6822001 rubric remains applicable:
- Local continuous ASR and partial text: implemented; validate longer sessions.
- Real user/problem: recruit relevant volunteers who would actually use a voice journal.
- Technical evaluation: calculate ASR WER against checked references, compare clean
  speech with one experimental condition, measure responsiveness and analyze errors.
- User evaluation: at least five relevant real participants; observe speaking,
  correcting, self-labeling, saving and reopening. Ask whether the tone suggestion
  is helpful or misleading and whether storage/privacy behavior is understood.
- Report: Introduction, Fundamentals, System Design, Implementation, Technical
  Experiment, User Testing, Discussion, Conclusion; include evidence and AI usage
  log/declaration. Do not invent participant feedback or evaluation results.

Tests verify implementation behavior, not journaling benefit or well-being outcomes.
Acted-corpus emotion accuracy is not live-journal accuracy. Use non-sensitive test
entries and retain participants' personal content only with their permission.
